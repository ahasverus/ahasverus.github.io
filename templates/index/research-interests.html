As a data scientist, I support international working groups using large-scale biodiversity data to understand how species and ecosystems respond to environmental change, particularly climate and land-use change.

My work spans species distribution modelling, functional ecology, macroecology, and biodiversity dynamics across spatial and temporal scales.

I also develop R-based tools and reproducible workflows to support ecological data analysis and open science.
